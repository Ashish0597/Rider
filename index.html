06 // CLARIFICATIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED

How do I choose the correct helmet size?
Measure around the widest part of your head above your eyebrows. All helmets include removable 3D contour cheek pads to adjust between track snugness and touring comfort.
Are these helmets certified for track racing?
Yes, all Track-Spec helmets carry ECE 22.06 and FIM homologation labels compliant with international sanctioning bodies.
What warranty covers crash damage?
5-year structural warranty, plus 50% crash-replacement coverage within the first 24 months.
