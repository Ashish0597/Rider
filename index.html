06 // CLARIFICATIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED

How do I choose the correct helmet size?
Measure around the largest part of your head (about 1 inch above your eyebrows). Our helmets have exchangeable 3D cheek pads to customize the fit from snug track to touring comfort.
Are these helmets certified for competitive racing?
Yes, all Track-Spec variants carry both ECE 22.06 and FIM homologation labels required by international racing circuits.
What warranty covers crash damage?
We offer a 5-year structural warranty, plus a 50% crash-replacement discount if your helmet is involved in an incident within the first 24 months.
